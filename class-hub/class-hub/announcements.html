Semester 3 kickoff — here's what's on our radar
Hi everyone — welcome to Semester 3! We hope you all had a chance to recharge and are feeling ready to go. A few things we want to make sure are on your radar as we get started.

Dr. Julien will be hosting a weekly study session open to the cohort — this is something she's generously offering on her own time, separate from any course. She's adjusted the time to Tuesdays at 2:00 PM EST to avoid conflicting with the end of our 753 class. We know so many of you are excited for this opportunity, so come ready to engage and bring your questions. The Zoom link is on the hub.

Grand Rounds kicks off at 12:00 PM EST each week. Worth noting that our first NURS 733 lecture follows at 1:00 PM, so make sure you're tuned in and ready for both.

On the merch front, we're working on getting cohort gear together! If everything goes to plan, designs will be ready for you to browse by end of August / first week of September, with orders closing the first week of October and everything shipped back in time for Intensives Week in November.

In preparation for the White Coat Ceremony, what we're hoping to do is have your photo and some personal details displayed on screen as your name is called. I'll be reaching out soon with more details on what you'll need to submit — just something to start thinking about for now!

More to come as always — we're here if you need anything.
